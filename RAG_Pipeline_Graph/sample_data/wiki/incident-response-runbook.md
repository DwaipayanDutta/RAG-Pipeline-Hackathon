# Incident response runbook

## Declaring a SEV1 incident

To declare a SEV1 incident, page the on-call engineer of the affected service, open an incident
channel in Slack, and assign an incident commander. A SEV1 incident means customers cannot pay
or check out. Post a status update every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved.

## After the incident

Write a post-mortem within five working days. The post-mortem must list the timeline, the root
cause, and follow-up actions with owners.
